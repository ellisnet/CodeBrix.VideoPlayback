THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE NOTICES AND INFORMATION

CodeBrix.VideoPlayback is a video playback library for .NET: it reads WebM/MKV
and .cbv containers carrying AV1 video with Opus or Vorbis audio, exposes a
codec-neutral decoder seam, and presents frames through a Skia companion
package. CodeBrix.VideoPlayback does not incorporate any third-party source
code into its own assemblies: its container readers are written from the
published specifications (RFC 8794, RFC 9559, the WebM Container Guidelines
and the AV1/Opus/Vorbis-in-Matroska mappings).
