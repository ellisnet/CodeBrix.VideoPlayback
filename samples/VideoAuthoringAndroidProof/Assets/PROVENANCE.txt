PROVENANCE - samples/VideoAuthoringAndroidProof/Assets
=====================================================

Every file here is SYNTHETIC: no camera footage, no recording, no third-party
content. Pictures come from ffmpeg's built-in lavfi pattern generators
(testsrc / testsrc2 / smptehdbars), sound from its built-in "sine" generator,
and the lookup table from a short script. Licence: none needed - generated for
this repository and dedicated to the public domain (CC0-1.0) by its author.
Made on the development laptop (Debian ffmpeg 7.1) on 2026-10-12. The app
bundles them as Android assets and copies them into its files dir at start.

clip-h264-aac.mp4
    COPIED unchanged from CodeBrix.VideoProcessing's tests/Assets (its
    PROVENANCE.txt is the authority; reproduced here). 4 s, 320x240, 30 fps
    (120 frames), H.264 High 4:2:0 8-bit, a keyframe every 30 frames; AAC-LC
    stereo 48 kHz 96 kbit/s; moov before mdat; no metadata tags.

    ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=320x240:rate=30:duration=4 \
           -f lavfi -i sine=frequency=440:sample_rate=48000:duration=4 \
           -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 30 -keyint_min 30 \
           -sc_threshold 0 -bf 2 -crf 23 \
           -c:a aac -b:a 96k -ac 2 \
           -map_metadata -1 -fflags +bitexact -flags:v +bitexact \
           -flags:a +bitexact -movflags +faststart clip-h264-aac.mp4

clip-rotated90.mp4
    clip-h264-aac.mp4 with a display-matrix rotation added (streams copied, no
    re-encode). ffprobe reports "rotation=90" (anticlockwise); the Android
    engine reports it as 270 degrees clockwise. The source of step 06's
    AutoRotate shaping.

    ffmpeg -display_rotation 90 -i clip-h264-aac.mp4 -map 0 -c copy \
           -map_metadata -1 -movflags +faststart clip-rotated90.mp4

backdrop.png
    One 320x180 frame of SMPTE HD colour bars - the IMAGE backdrop of step 09.

    ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "smptehdbars=size=320x180" -frames:v 1 \
           -map_metadata -1 -fflags +bitexact -flags +bitexact backdrop.png

backdrop-3s.mp4
    The VIDEO backdrop of steps 09 and 10: 3 s, 320x180, 12 fps, H.264 High,
    AAC-LC stereo 48 kHz carrying a 660 Hz tone (so a Silent master's export
    takes an audibly different sound). Deliberately LONGER than
    alpha-master.cbvmaster (1 s), so that export's tail is backdrop only.

    ffmpeg -f lavfi -i "testsrc=size=320x180:rate=12:duration=3" \
           -f lavfi -i "sine=frequency=660:sample_rate=48000:duration=3" \
           -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 12 -crf 28 \
           -c:a aac -b:a 96k -ac 2 \
           -map_metadata -1 -fflags +bitexact -flags:v +bitexact \
           -flags:a +bitexact -movflags +faststart backdrop-3s.mp4

twist.cube
    A 5x5x5 "identity with a twist" 3-D lookup table (title
    "VAProof identity-with-twist"): out = (min(1, 0.9 R + 0.1), G, 0.85 B) -
    reds lifted a little, blues pulled down, green untouched. Written by this
    Python 3 snippet (red varies fastest, as .cube requires):

    n = 5
    lines = ['TITLE "VAProof identity-with-twist"', 'LUT_3D_SIZE 5',
             'DOMAIN_MIN 0.0 0.0 0.0', 'DOMAIN_MAX 1.0 1.0 1.0']
    for b in range(n):
        for g in range(n):
            for r in range(n):
                R, G, B = r / (n - 1), g / (n - 1), b / (n - 1)
                lines.append('%.6f %.6f %.6f' % (min(1.0, R * 0.9 + 0.1), G, B * 0.85))
    open('twist.cube', 'w').write('\n'.join(lines) + '\n')

alpha-master.cbvmaster
    COPIED unchanged from this repository's tests/assets/av1-alpha-flac.cbvmaster
    (tests/assets/ASSETS.txt, "MASTER (MODE3) FIXTURES", is the authority),
    SHA-256 037eed6e5f090078249a8e1575bb083ee1eabebcf57b6320c4c36f8da72251db.
    A Mode3 master WITH an alpha plane, authored on the HOST by
    CodeBrix.VideoPlayback.Authoring's own Master flavour:

    dotnet run --project tools/CodeBrix.VideoPlayback.AssetAuthoring \
               -c Release -- --master-fixtures

    from a lossless synthetic source (lavfi testsrc2=size=128x72:rate=12:
    duration=1, format=yuva420p, alpha = a horizontal gradient 255*X/(W-1);
    sound sine 440 Hz 48 kHz stereo; FFV1 + PCM in Matroska). 3 tracks: AV1
    128x72 I420 8-bit, an AV1 Gray 8-bit alpha plane, FLAC 48 kHz stereo
    16-bit; 12 frames, 1 s.
    WHY IT IS MADE ON THE HOST: authoring a master WITH alpha needs a source
    the engine can decode WITH its alpha channel (AlphaFrameSource). The
    Android engine cannot (MediaCodec delivers no alpha, and decodes no VP9
    alpha either), so alpha masters are authored where an alpha-capable source
    exists and only EXPORTED on the device.
